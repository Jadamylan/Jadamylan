# Mensis

> A menstrual cycle you can see, hear, and feel.

Mensis is an interactive data artwork created for a one-day creative data hackathon. It translates menstrual-cycle research into a calm, tactile experience: the moon becomes the clock, a glass becomes the measure, and sound becomes another way to perceive change over time.

[Open the hackathon preview](https://preview--pixel-moon-canvas.lovable.app/?__lovable_receipt=mat_2br5z9d9gb855tpz6xt5g68v2e)

## The idea

A visitor drags a ring of imperfect moon phases to move through a menstrual cycle. The ring is intentionally lopsided because no two cycles are identical; a faint dashed 28-day circle remains behind it as a reference, not a rule.

Beside the ring, a realistic glass fills as the illustrative menstrual flow model accumulates. At the end of the cycle, the glass pours out before the experience returns to Day 1. The pour is a poetic transition—not a biological claim.

## What visitors can do

- Move through the cycle by dragging the moon, using arrow keys, or pressing play.
- Compare a research-based mcPHASES profile with clearly labeled illustrative short, typical, and longer/heavier cycles.
- See the current day, phase, daily illustrative flow, cumulative volume, and vessel equivalents.
- Hear an artistic sonification of cycle position and phase using Web Audio.
- Select a cycle day and symptoms to receive a short, non-diagnostic explanation generated with Mistral AI.
- Scan a QR code to open the experience on a phone.

## Data and creative layers

- **Cala** helped identify and structure research and clinical sources.
- **mcPHASES** supplies research context on multimodal cycle data, including hormonal, physiological, and self-reported measures.
- **ACOG** supplies adolescent clinical guidance, including the 21–45 day cycle interval and bleeding lasting 7 days or fewer.
- **Krea** produced the realistic moon, glass, linen, and water imagery.
- **Lovable** was used to build and host the interactive experience.
- **Mistral AI** powers the optional educational phase explanation.

## Responsible interpretation

Mensis separates three kinds of information in the interface:

1. **Research data** — findings reported by cited studies.
2. **Clinical guidance** — authoritative educational ranges.
3. **Illustrative models** — the daily flow shape, vessel marker, moon mapping, and sound design.

The moon is a cultural and linguistic metaphor, not a prediction system. Menstrual cycles do not reliably synchronize with lunar phases. The AI explanation is educational and non-diagnostic. It does not replace medical care.

Mensis does not collect, predict, or store personal menstrual data. Only the selected day, cycle length, displayed phase, selected symptoms, and profile type are sent for an explanation.

## Design principles

- Warm, calm, realistic, and unembarrassed.
- Inclusive of everyone who menstruates.
- No euphemisms or default pink period branding.
- Keyboard accessible, touch friendly, mobile responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Clear labeling wherever the artwork moves from measured data into modeling or metaphor.

Illustrative and educational, not medical advice. If symptoms are severe, unusual, or concerning, a qualified healthcare professional can provide individualized guidance.
